# Fortnightly newsletter emails (for Spinalogic)

Each blog post goes live on the website on its date. On that day (or the morning after),
send the matching email below from **Spinalogic → Email marketing** to your newsletter group.

**Who to send to:** patients who have agreed to receive emails (see the consent note in
`BLOG-AND-NEWSLETTER.md`). Every email must include an unsubscribe option: check Spinalogic adds one, or add a line such as "Reply STOP to unsubscribe".

Copy the subject and body as they are. The link opens the full article on your website.
