As the new school year approaches, it’s a great time to think about how your kids carry their bags, sit at their desks and use their devices.

## School bags

A heavy or poorly fitted bag can pull on a child’s shoulders and change the way they stand and walk. Look for:

- **Weight:** as a general guide, a loaded bag should be no more than about 10% of your child’s body weight.
- **Two straps, both shoulders:** slinging a bag over one shoulder loads one side.
- **Padded, adjustable straps** that keep the bag sitting high and close to the back, not hanging down near the bottom.
- **A waist or chest strap** for heavier loads.
- **Pack smart:** heaviest items closest to the back, and only what’s needed for the day.

## Screens and homework

Kids often curl up on couches, beds and floors with laptops and tablets. Encourage:

- a desk or table for homework where possible
- the screen raised closer to eye level
- feet supported on the floor or a box
- a movement break every 20–30 minutes

## Keep them moving

Active play, sport, swimming and time outdoors are the best things for growing bodies. Aim for at least an hour of physical activity a day.

## Signs worth checking

If your child often complains of back or neck pain, headaches, or you notice one shoulder sitting higher than the other, it’s worth getting them assessed. We see children of all ages and tailor our approach to each child.
