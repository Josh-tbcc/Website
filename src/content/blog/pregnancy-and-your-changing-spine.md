Your body changes a lot during pregnancy, and your spine and pelvis carry much of that load. Many mums-to-be notice back, hip or pelvic discomfort at some stage.

## What’s changing

- **Your centre of gravity shifts forward** as your baby grows, increasing the curve in your lower back.
- **Hormones like relaxin** loosen ligaments to prepare your pelvis for birth, which can make joints feel less stable.
- **Your posture adapts** to the extra weight at the front, which can load your upper back, neck and shoulders too.
- **Sleep gets harder**, which affects how well your body recovers.

## Simple ways to stay comfortable

1. **Keep moving.** Walking, swimming and pregnancy-specific exercise classes are great options. Check with your midwife or doctor first.
2. **Sleep on your side** with a pillow between your knees, and another supporting your bump if needed.
3. **Avoid standing on one leg** for tasks like getting dressed. Sit down to put on pants and shoes.
4. **Roll onto your side** to get out of bed, rather than sitting straight up.
5. **Wear supportive, low shoes.**

## How we adapt care in pregnancy

We use pregnancy-friendly positioning and gentle techniques suited to each stage of pregnancy. We have specially designed pregnancy pillows that let mums-to-be lie comfortably and safely on their tummy, which many find a real relief. We always work alongside your midwife or doctor.

## When to seek medical advice

Contact your midwife or doctor straight away if you have bleeding, severe or sudden pain, fluid loss, reduced baby movements, or any symptoms that worry you.
