Big new year’s resolutions often fade by February. Small habits, repeated every day, are what really change how you feel. Here are five we recommend to our practice members.

## 1. Move first thing

Before you check your phone, spend two minutes moving: a few gentle stretches, some squats, or a short walk outside. It wakes up your body and sets the tone for the day.

## 2. Break up sitting

Set a reminder to stand up and move every 30–45 minutes. Walk to fill your water bottle, take phone calls standing, or do a lap of the office.

## 3. Walk most days

Walking is one of the simplest and most effective things you can do for your back, your heart and your mood. Aim for 20–30 minutes. The Sunshine Coast makes it easy, from rail trails to the beach.

## 4. Look after your sleep

Go to bed and get up at similar times each day, keep your room cool and dark, and check that your pillow keeps your neck in line with your spine.

## 5. Get regular check-ups

You check your teeth and your car regularly. Your spine and nervous system deserve the same attention, ideally before pain forces the issue.

## Make it easy

Pick one habit and do it every day for two weeks before adding another. Small wins build momentum.
