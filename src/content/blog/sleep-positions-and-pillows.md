We spend around a third of our lives asleep, so how we sleep matters. If you often wake up with a stiff neck, sore shoulders or a headache, your sleep setup is worth a look.

## The best positions

**On your back:** keeps your spine in a fairly neutral position. A medium-height pillow that supports the curve of your neck works well, and a pillow under your knees can ease lower back pressure.

**On your side:** the most common position. Choose a pillow that fills the gap between your ear and shoulder so your head stays level. A pillow between your knees keeps your hips and lower back aligned.

**On your stomach:** this turns your head to one side for hours and can strain your neck and lower back. If you can, try gradually transitioning to your side.

## Choosing a pillow

There’s no single “best” pillow. It depends on your size and how you sleep. Look for one that:

- keeps your head in line with your spine
- isn’t so high it tilts your head up, or so flat your head drops
- still feels supportive after a few months (most pillows need replacing every 1–2 years)

## Your mattress

A mattress that sags or is more than 8–10 years old may not be giving you the support you need.

## Other sleep tips

- Keep a regular bedtime and wake time.
- Put screens away 30 minutes before bed.
- Keep your bedroom cool and dark, especially through the Queensland summer.
