End-of-year work deadlines, school concerts, Christmas shopping, parties and travel. December on the Sunshine Coast is fun, but it’s also one of the most stressful months of the year.

## Where stress shows up in your body

When you’re under pressure, your nervous system shifts towards “fight or flight”. Muscles tighten, breathing gets shallower and sleep often suffers. For many people that shows up as:

- tight neck and shoulders
- tension headaches
- a stiff, achy upper back
- clenching or a sore jaw
- feeling flat and run down

## Protect your sleep

Sleep is when your body recovers. Over the festive season:

- Try to keep a regular bedtime, even after a late night.
- Limit screens for the last 30 minutes before bed.
- Go easy on alcohol and caffeine in the evening.
- Check your pillow keeps your neck in line with your spine.

## Keep moving

It’s tempting to skip exercise when you’re busy, but even a 15-minute walk helps your body handle stress. Morning walks before the heat kicks in are perfect at this time of year.

## Breathe

Try this when you feel wound up: breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds, hold for 4, and breathe out slowly for 6. Repeat for a minute. It helps calm your nervous system.
